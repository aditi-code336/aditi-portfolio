Projects

Bhumikarta

Second place · IIT BHU

A real-time dashboard that brings market, weather and crop-health data together for farmers. It supports voice-powered queries in local languages and AI-driven insights. I worked on the frontend and UI/UX, aiming for clearer, more accessible ways for farmers to use data.

Read the ticket

Problem: useful farming information is scattered. Approach: one dashboard for market, weather and crop health, with voice queries in local languages. My contribution: frontend and UI/UX. Outcome: second place at IIT BHU.

PawPrint

Dec 2025 to ongoing · Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs

A wildlife conservation tracking system. I'm developing the backend that manages species, habitat and incident data across national parks, exposed through REST APIs for structured access and monitoring. It's designed around rangers who work in low-connectivity areas, with offline field entry that syncs when a network returns.

Read the ticket

Problem: park data lives across logbooks, messages and spreadsheets. Approach: a central backend with offline-first field entry. Built with: Java Spring Boot and REST APIs. Status: ongoing.

Rupaantar & Shilpkaar

Enactus MPSTME · Project Head (Rupaantar)

Two social enterprises built on one idea: waste becomes material, material becomes an artisan product, and the product creates livelihood. Rupaantar turns agricultural by-products such as rice straw into handmade paper, notebooks, journals and wall art, and has been through hands-on prototyping sessions, including the Aaroh collection. Shilpkaar works with artisans on upcycled plastic products. My work covers product development, costing, marketing and outreach.
